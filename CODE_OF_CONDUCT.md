# Code of conduct

This is a short form of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The Covenant is itself under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The full text, including enforcement guidelines, is at that link. This page is the working version for readers of this archive.

## Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this archive a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, and healthy community.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that helps this archive:

- Showing empathy and kindness.
- Respecting differing opinions, even when the historical argument is sharp.
- Giving and accepting correction gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the readers and for the people whose lives are being written about.
- Taking responsibility when a mistake is pointed out.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexual language or imagery, and sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others’ private information, such as a home address, without their permission.
- Inventing quotations, dates, or anecdotes about Dr. Kurien or about living people.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Kurien’s life includes arguments about politics, religion, and institutions. Those arguments can be written. They cannot be used as a pretext to abuse a contributor, a family member, or a farmer.

## Where it applies

This code applies in GitHub issues and in any other space where someone is contributing to this archive. It also applies when a person is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported by opening a GitHub issue marked as a conduct report, or by writing to the maintainers through whatever contact the repository page gives. If you are not safe putting the report in public, say so in the first line and share only what a maintainer needs in order to reply in private.

Maintainers will respect the privacy of the person who reports. They may remove comments, commits, or issues that break this code, and they may ban a contributor for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
