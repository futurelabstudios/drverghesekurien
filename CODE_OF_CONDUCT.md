# Code of Conduct

This archive honours a man who believed in the dignity of every farmer. We ask contributors to extend the same dignity to each other.

- Be respectful of every contributor, whatever their language, background or view of Dr. Kurien.
- Disagree with evidence, not insults.
- Respect privacy and consent, especially for living people and families.
- No harassment, hate speech, spam or promotion.
- Don't upload content you don't have rights to.

Maintainers may edit, decline or remove contributions, and block people who repeatedly break these rules. Report concerns by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers privately.
