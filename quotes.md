# Quotes

Short lines only. Each one has a listener and a link. Speeches are not reproduced.

## In his own mouth, as a journalist or a colleague wrote it down

**On staying.** “That happiness was infectious and I caught it. Fifty years later, I have never left Anand and have no desire to leave.” Said of Tribhuvandas Patel, to National Institute of Design graduates, reported by Rediff on 8 December 1998. [Source](https://www.rediff.com/business/1998/dec/08kurien.htm)

**On the brand.** “Without a brand, you will just be a contract supplier… The cream is not there. It lies in marketing.” From an interview around 2004, quoted in *The Hindu Business Line*, 9 September 2012. [Source](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/opinion/The-man-who-empowered-through-milk/article20499872.ece)

**On the point of the work.** “I am in the business of empowerment. Milk is just a tool in that.” The same profile prints this as his line. [Source](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/opinion/The-man-who-empowered-through-milk/article20499872.ece)

**On the World Food Prize.** He said he was delighted, and that the prize was really for the heroic efforts of six million farmers, whose elected chairman he was. The prize foundation prints the remark on his laureate page. [Source](https://www.worldfoodprize.org/blog/people/dr-verghese-kurien/)

**On method, at NDDB.** “People will tell you there are many ways to skin a cat. But here in NDDB, we have only one — the Amul pattern.” Tushaar Shah, recalling 1977. [Source](https://vrikshamandir.com/2026/09/17/remembering-kurien-the-man-who-changed-countless-lives/)

**On Dalaya.** “There is a clear division of labour. In Amul days, Dalaya did the work, and I did all the talking.” Shah, quoting him. [Source](https://vrikshamandir.com/2026/09/17/remembering-kurien-the-man-who-changed-countless-lives/)

**On the word he wanted.** At IRMA’s first convocation he stopped a Gujarati translation that had turned “employee” into “servant,” and took Indira Gandhi’s word, *karmachari*. The scene is told in full in [Stories](stories/anecdotes.md). [Source](https://vrikshamandir.com/2026/09/17/remembering-kurien-the-man-who-changed-countless-lives/)

**On a king who asked to see him.** “Cows. What else?” To Rasheeda Bhagat, about the King of Thailand. [Source](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/doodh-ka-doodh-kurien-style/article23032255.ece)

**On a multinational.** “It is about time the white man understood that all Indians are not for sale.” To Bhagat, about a Sri Lanka venture. [Source](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/doodh-ka-doodh-kurien-style/article23032255.ece)

**On integrity, as his staff remembered the refrain.** “Integrity, integrity and integrity.” P. S. Mohan, IRMA *Network*, December 2012. [Source](https://www.irma.ac.in/faculty-research/research-and-publications/network/current-and-past-issues?issueid=180&tab=2)

**On what he expected of students, late in life.** “Be true Irmans.” Arpit Shah, PRM 30, reporting a meeting on 8 April during his course. [Source](http://joy-of-books.blogspot.com/2006/04/kurien-personal-essay.html)

**To a student who reached for his feet.** No one is greater than the other. Ajay Gupta, June 2012. [Source](https://vrikshamandir.com/2020/05/30/four-occasions-four-memories-and-four-messages/)

## Ten pieces of advice

At the same NID convocation in 1998 he offered ten rules. Rediff listed them. Paraphrased, they were: work on a scale so large that failure cannot be admitted; have the experience and the skill the job needs; call on others when you must, and on your own terms; be clear about the aim; know the market’s need, rather than the need you prefer to imagine; listen to consumers; respect them; never compromise on quality; work with people who are committed, and keep them young in energy; remember that honesty is the only policy, because it gives strength and humility, and listen to wisdom from any quarter.

[Source](https://www.rediff.com/business/1998/dec/08kurien.htm)

## Lines about him, from people who worked with him

**Amrita Patel**, 12 September 2012, on what he combined: ideology, professionalism, integrity, and business sense. [Source](http://www.rediff.com/money/report/interview-amrita-patel-on-kurien-ideologies/20120912.htm)

**Shyam Benegal**, 9 September 2012: Kurien would often say he was a servant of the farmers, and he meant it. Set this next to the convocation story. He rejected *sevak* in Gujarati on the Amul lawns, and he used “servant” in English with the film-maker. [Source](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india/how-a-farmers-servant-painted-the-nation-white/story-w5muyu4dpzbC3Huk5XYRbK.html)

**Nirmala Kurien**, 26 November 2018: he worked his whole life as an employee of the farmers. [Source](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/he-was-an-atheist-religion-played-no-part-in-his-life-says-kuriens-daughter-5466180/)

**Norman Borlaug**, as the World Food Prize recorded it in 2012: one of the world’s great agricultural leaders of the twentieth century. [Source](https://www.worldfoodprize.org/blog/2012/09/10/the-world-food-prize-pays-tribute-to-1989-laureate-dr-verghese-kurien/)
