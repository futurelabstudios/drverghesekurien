# Dr. Verghese Kurien — The People's Archive

**An open-source, community-built archive of the life, institutions, ideas and memories of Dr. Verghese Kurien (26 November 1921 – 9 September 2012), the "Father of India's White Revolution."**

He was a mechanical engineer who was sent to a government creamery in Anand in 1949 and never left. Over the next six decades he helped build **Amul**, the **National Dairy Development Board (NDDB)**, **Operation Flood**, the **Gujarat Co-operative Milk Marketing Federation (GCMMF)** and the **Institute of Rural Management Anand (IRMA)**. Together they put farmers in charge of their own produce and helped make India the world's largest milk producer.

This archive is a **living, public report**. Anyone can add to it: a story, a photo, a letter, a video, a correction or a source.

- **[Photos](media/photos/)**, **[Videos](media/videos/)**, **[Documents](media/documents/)** — catalogued with source, date, place, people and rights information.

## How to contribute

Contributions arrive as **pull requests** and are reviewed by maintainers before they go live. Never used GitHub? You can still do it entirely in your browser.

1. Read the **[Contributing guide](CONTRIBUTING.md)**.
2. Copy the right template: [memory](memories/_TEMPLATE.md) or [media](media/_TEMPLATE.md).
3. Open a pull request. A maintainer will check sources, consent and rights, then merge.

Every fact in the report should point to a source. Every memory should name its teller (or say why it can't). Every photo should say who owns it.
