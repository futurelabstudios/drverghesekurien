# Dr. Verghese Kurien: a public archive

This is a reading archive about Dr. Verghese Kurien (26 November 1921 – 9 September 2012), the dairy engineer who helped India’s farmers build Amul, the National Dairy Development Board, and the Institute of Rural Management Anand. Nothing has to be installed. Open any file in a browser or a text editor.

The priority chapter is the collection of stories:

**[Stories and anecdotes](stories/anecdotes.md)**

Each story names who told it, and links to the public page where this archive found it. The writing is original paraphrase. It does not copy articles, speeches, book chapters, or Wikipedia.

## How to read the stories

A story is included when a named person, an institution he built, or a journalist who interviewed him has put it on a public page, and this research opened that page. Where two honest accounts differ — a number of Amul unions, a starting salary, the name of an American campus — both are kept, and neither is silently “corrected.”

Indian dates are written day, month, year: 26 November 1921; he died on 9 September 2012.
