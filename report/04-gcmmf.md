# 4. Gujarat Co-operative Milk Marketing Federation (GCMMF)

## What it is

GCMMF is the apex marketing body of Gujarat's district milk unions. It owns and markets the **Amul** brand; the district unions own the plants and procure milk from village societies.

## Founding (1973)

- Set up in **1973**, in the year of the Kaira Union's 25th anniversary celebrations.
- Purpose: stop Gujarat's district unions competing against each other in the market and pool their strength behind **one brand**. The Kaira Union transferred the Amul brand to GCMMF.
- **Kurien was chairman from its founding in 1973 until 20 March 2006.**

## How it works

- Farmer → village society → district union → GCMMF → distributors → consumer.
- GCMMF does not own processing plants; it plans, markets and distributes.
- Its managing directors have been professionals; for many years these included IRMA graduates — Kurien's "rural managers" in practice.

## The 2006 exit

In March 2006, after a no-confidence move by the GCMMF board and a Gujarat High Court order on co-opted members of cooperative boards, Kurien resigned. He said: "My decision is in deference to the recent order of the Gujarat High Court" and that he was "anguished by a recent move of the GCMMF board against me." See [Chapter 8](08-conflicts.md).

## Today

Amul/GCMMF reports roughly **3.6 million** member producers and turnover of about **₹90,000 crore** (FY25).
