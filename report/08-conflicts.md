# 8. Conflicts & controversies

An honest archive records the hard parts too. Please contribute **sourced** material and keep the tone fair to everyone involved.

## Against multinationals (1950s–1990s)

- 1956: refused by Nestlé on technology; expanded Amul's own production.
- 1962: lobbied to restrain Polson's lines when Amul diverted output to the armed forces.
- 1990s: after liberalisation in 1991, lobbied against multinational entry into dairying.

## Differences with NDDB's leadership (late 1990s–2000s)

Kurien persuaded PM Atal Bihari Vajpayee to appoint **Dr. Amrita Patel** as his successor at NDDB in 1998. They later disagreed sharply: Kurien felt some NDDB policies weakened cooperatives by opening marketing to private players and corporatised structures.

## IRMA, 2005

The dispute over his lifetime chairmanship of IRMA. Full account in [Chapter 5 → The 2005 crisis](05-irma.md#the-2005-crisis).

## GCMMF, 2006

- GCMMF's board moved a no-confidence motion and approached authorities to stop him functioning as chairman.
- A Gujarat High Court order on co-opted members of cooperative management committees affected his position.
- On **20 March 2006** he resigned after 33 years as chairman, saying he was "anguished" and asking whether he deserved "this kind of treatment" after four decades of service.

## Criticism of his style

Colleagues and critics described him as autocratic and uncompromising; supporters describe him as demanding, principled and personally kind. Both views appear in the sources and both belong here.
