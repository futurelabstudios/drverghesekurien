# 6. Other institutions & ventures

Kurien applied the Anand pattern — farmer ownership plus professional management — well beyond milk.

| Institution / venture | Year | What it did | Status of our info |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Indian Dairy Corporation (IDC)** | 1970 | Received and managed Operation Flood food aid; merged into NDDB in 1987 | ✅ |
| **National Cooperative Dairy Federation of India (NCDFI)**, Anand | c. 1970 | National apex body of dairy cooperatives; coordinates marketing and policy | ⚠️ founding year to confirm |
| **Mother Dairy**, Delhi | 1974 | NDDB subsidiary created under Operation Flood to supply milk to Delhi; famous token-operated bulk vending booths | ⚠️ confirm year |
| **Oilseeds Growers' Cooperative Project ("Operation Golden Flow")** | 1979 | Cooperatives for oilseed farmers; 500,000+ farmers by the 1980s | ✅ |
| **Dhara** edible oil | 1988 | NDDB's edible oil brand, created to stabilise prices and counter adulteration | ✅ |
| **Fruit & vegetables (Safal)** | 1980s | Cooperative model applied to fruit and vegetables; today Mother Dairy Fruit & Vegetable Pvt. Ltd. | ⚠️ details wanted |
| **Indian Immunologicals Ltd.**, Hyderabad | 1980s | Animal vaccines (NDDB subsidiary) | ⚠️ year to confirm |
| **Indian Dairy Machinery Company (IDMC)** | — | Indigenous dairy equipment (NDDB subsidiary) | ⚠️ year to confirm |
| **Tribhuvandas Foundation**, Anand | — | NGO for women's and children's health in Kheda; Kurien was chairman | ✅ |
| **IRMA** | 1979 | See [Chapter 5](05-irma.md) | ✅ |

Other ventures often credited to Kurien's circle — salt farmers' cooperatives, tree growers' cooperatives, and others — need sources. **If you have them, please contribute.**
