# The Report

A sourced, chapter-by-chapter account of Dr. Verghese Kurien's life and the institutions he built. Every chapter ends with its sources. Where sources disagree, we say so rather than pick one silently.

**Conventions**

- ✅ **Verified** — supported by at least one reliable published source listed in the chapter.
- ⚠️ **Sources differ** — we show both versions and ask for help in [Open questions](12-open-questions.md).
- 🗣️ **Memory** — a first-hand account; see the [Memories](../memories/) collection.
