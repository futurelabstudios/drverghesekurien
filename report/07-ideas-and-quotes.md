# 7. Ideas, philosophy & quotes

## Core ideas

1. **Farmers must own the institutions that handle their produce.** Ownership, not charity, changes lives.
2. **Professionals must work for farmers.** The elected farmer board governs; managers manage.
3. **Rural does not mean backward.** Rural organisations deserve the best managers and technology.
4. **Self-reliance.** From buffalo-milk powder to Indian dairy machinery, he resisted dependence on multinationals and imports.
5. **Institutions, not individuals.** He built bodies designed to outlive him — NDDB, GCMMF, IRMA.
6. **Development is done, not written about.** He was impatient with theory that never met a village.
