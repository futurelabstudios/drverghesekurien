## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Born** | 26 November 1921, Calicut (now Kozhikode, Kerala), Madras Presidency |
| **Died** | 9 September 2012, Nadiad (near Anand), Gujarat, aged 90 |
| **Family** | Wife Molly; daughter Nirmala; a grandson |
| **Training** | B.Sc. Physics (Loyola College, 1940); B.E. Mechanical (College of Engineering, Guindy, 1943); M.S. Mechanical Engineering, Michigan State University (1948; minor given as dairy engineering by Amul, nuclear physics by MSU) |
| **Called** | "Father of the White Revolution"; the "Milkman of India" |
| **Karma bhumi** | Anand, Gujarat — for more than six decades |
| **Remembered on** | **National Milk Day, 26 November** (his birthday) |

## Early life

Kurien was born into a Malayali Syrian Christian family. His father was a government civil surgeon in Cochin; his mother is described by IRMA's former director Katar Singh as highly educated. He went to school at the Diamond Jubilee Higher Secondary School in Gobichettipalayam, where his father worked at the government hospital. He was raised a Christian and later described himself as an atheist.

## Education and the accidental dairy engineer

- **1940** — graduates in physics from Loyola College, Madras.
- **1943** — bachelor's in mechanical engineering from the College of Engineering, Guindy (University of Madras).
- Works at the **Tata Steel Technical Institute, Jamshedpur** after hoping to join the Army as an engineer.
- Applies for a government scholarship in metallurgical engineering and is instead awarded one in **dairy engineering** — the accident that shaped Indian dairying.
- Trains at the **Imperial Institute of Animal Husbandry, Bangalore**.
- Arrives at **Michigan State University** in the winter of 1946, by the university's own 2018 account. He debates with fellow students and wins a tennis championship.
- Earns a master's in mechanical engineering at Michigan State. ⚠️ Sources give **1947** and **1948**; MSU (2018) and *The Hindu* (2016) both say 1948. ⚠️ Amul gives the minor as dairy engineering; MSU says nuclear physics. He later joked that he studied metallurgical and nuclear engineering on the dairy scholarship.

## Anand

- **1946** — farmers of Kaira (Kheda) district, led by **Tribhuvandas Patel** and advised by **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel** and **Morarji Desai**, form the Kaira District Co-operative Milk Producers' Union, registered **14 December 1946**, to escape the middleman Polson.
- **1949** — the government posts Kurien to its experimental creamery at Anand to work off his scholarship bond. He wants to leave. In the evenings he helps Tribhuvandas Patel fix the cooperative's equipment. Patel persuades him to stay.
- Nobody would rent to him at first — he was a Christian, a meat-eater and a bachelor — so he lived in a garage for a long time (as recalled in *Business Today*, 2000).
- **1950** — becomes General Manager of the Kaira Union.
- **1948–1952** — daily procurement grows from about 200 litres to 20,000 litres.
- His Michigan classmate **H. M. Dalaya** joins him and develops the world's first process for making **skim milk powder and condensed milk from buffalo milk** — a breakthrough for India, where buffalo milk dominated.
- **1955** — the Kaira Union starts using the brand name **Amul**.
- **1956** — visits Nestlé in Switzerland. Nestlé refuses to share technology; he returns and expands condensed milk production. Two years later India bans condensed milk imports.
- **1962** — during the war with China, Amul diverts production to the armed forces.
- **1963** — Ramon Magsaysay Award for Community Leadership.
- **October 1964** — PM **Lal Bahadur Shastri** inaugurates Amul's cattle feed factory, spends a night in a village, and asks Kurien to replicate the "Anand pattern" across India.

## National builder

- **16 July 1965** — **NDDB** is founded at Anand, with Kurien as chairman. Same year: Padma Shri and an honorary degree from Michigan State.
- **1966** — Padma Bhushan. The **Amul girl** is born (Sylvester daCunha's agency).
- **1970** — **Operation Flood** begins.
- **1973** — **GCMMF** is formed to market the produce of Gujarat's district unions under one brand, Amul. Kurien is its chairman from founding until 2006.
- **1976** — Shyam Benegal's *Manthan*, funded by 500,000 farmers giving ₹2 each, is released.
- **14 December 1979** — **IRMA** is founded. Kurien is its Founder Chairman. Same year: Oilseeds Growers' Cooperative Project ("Operation Golden Flow").
- **1982** — PM **Indira Gandhi** is chief guest at IRMA's first convocation.
- **1986** — Wateler Peace Prize; Krishi Ratna.
- **12 October 1987** — the NDDB Act, 1987 takes effect; NDDB merges with the Indian Dairy Corporation and becomes an institution of national importance.
- **1988** — the **Dhara** edible oil brand is launched.
- **1989** — **World Food Prize**.
- **1993** — International Person of the Year, World Dairy Expo.
- **1997** — Order of Agricultural Merit (France). First memoir-style book, *An Unfinished Dream*.
- **1998** — India overtakes the United States as the world's largest milk producer. Kurien steps down from NDDB; **Dr. Amrita Patel** succeeds him.
- **1999** — **Padma Vibhushan**.

## Later years

- **2001** — Economic Times Lifetime Achievement Award.
- **2005** — Publishes *I Too Had a Dream* (as told to Gouri Salvi). A bitter dispute over his chairmanship of IRMA reaches the courts (see [Chapter 5](05-irma.md#the-2005-crisis)).
- **2006** — Steps down as Founder Chairman of IRMA. On **20 March 2006** resigns as chairman of GCMMF after a no-confidence move by its board (see [Chapter 8](08-conflicts.md)).
- **2011** — Karmaveer Chakra.
- **9 September 2012** — dies in Nadiad aged 90.
- **2012** — IRMA institutes the annual **Dr. Verghese Kurien Memorial Lecture**; the first is delivered by Dr. M. S. Swaminathan.
- **2013** — Amar Chitra Katha publishes a comic on his life.
- **2014 onwards** — 26 November is observed as **National Milk Day**.
- **30 June 2016** — Michigan State installs his bust at its International Center, at the request of D. V. Mallikarjuna of the Shivamogga milk union ([*The Hindu*](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/Michigan-State-University-honours-Verghese-Kurien/article14477144.ece)).
- **Autumn 2017** — memorial talk at Michigan State, with Nirmala Kurien and R. S. Sodhi ([MSU](https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/seeking-equity-for-dairy-farmers-in-india-was-lifelong-legacy)).
- **2024** — the restored *Manthan* is screened at Cannes Classics.
- **2025** — IRMA becomes part of **Tribhuvan Sahkari University**, named after his mentor Tribhuvandas Patel (see [Chapter 5](05-irma.md#2025-irma-becomes-part-of-tribhuvan-sahkari-university)).
