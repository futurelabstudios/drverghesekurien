## *Manthan* (1976)

- Directed by **Shyam Benegal**; cinematography by **Govind Nihalani**; starring Girish Karnad, Smita Patil and Naseeruddin Shah. Inspired by Amul's story.
- Funded by about **500,000 farmers contributing ₹2 each** — called India's first crowdfunded film.
- Shown across Gujarat to raise awareness among milk producers.
- **2024:** restored from the original 35mm camera negative (NFDC–NFAI), funded by **GCMMF**, presented by the **Film Heritage Foundation** at **Cannes Classics**.

## The Amul girl (1966–)

Created by Sylvester daCunha's agency for Amul butter in 1966. Kurien defended the campaign's freedom to comment on current affairs, even when it angered the powerful.

## Television
