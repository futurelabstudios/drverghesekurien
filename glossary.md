# Glossary

**Ajarpura.** A village in the old Kaira district, about 15 kilometres from Anand. Its dairy cooperative was registered on 7 August 1947 with union code 0001. Lal Bahadur Shastri spent the night of 30 October 1964 there.

**Amul.** The brand of the Kaira union and, later, of the Gujarat Cooperative Milk Marketing Federation. Harish Damodaran writes that it is not an abbreviation of “Anand Milk Union Limited.” A house definition of the word was not on the pages this archive opened.

**Anand pattern.** The three-tier dairy cooperative Kurien and Tribhuvandas Patel built, and which NDDB was told to copy: a village society, a district union, and a marketing federation, with professionals employed by the farmers’ boards. Also called the Amul pattern.

**Anandalaya.** A school Kurien helped to found in 1988 for the children of people working in the Anand institutions.

**Butter oil.** Milk fat in a form that can be shipped as food aid. Together with skim milk powder, it was the European commodity Operation Flood sold in order to fund Indian dairies.

**Dhara.** The edible-oil brand Kurien attached to the oilseeds growers’ cooperatives.

**Flush and lean.** The months when animals give more milk than the liquid market will take, and the months when they give less. A powder plant was the cooperative’s way of moving milk from one to the other.

**GCMMF.** Gujarat Co-operative Milk Marketing Federation Ltd, formed in 1973. It markets the Amul brand for the district unions. Kurien chaired it from 1973 to 20 March 2006.

**H. M. Dalaya.** Harichand Megha Dalaya, the dairy technologist Kurien credited with the work of processing buffalo milk. Nirmala Kurien called him her father’s brother in the work.

**IRMA.** Institute of Rural Management Anand, founded in 1979 to train managers for cooperatives and, later, for a wider rural economy. Kurien was chairman from 1979 to 2006. The postgraduate programme is often called PRM. Alumni identify themselves by batch: “PRM 6 (1985–87).”

**Kaira / Kheda.** The district of which Anand is the centre. Kaira is the older English spelling, and the name in the union’s title. Kheda is the later official spelling.

**Karmachari / sevak.** At IRMA’s first convocation, a Gujarati interpreter rendered “employee of farmers” as *sevak* (servant). Kurien stopped him. Indira Gandhi supplied *karmachari* (employee). He wanted that word.

**Kaira District Co-operative Milk Producers’ Union.** The cooperative Tribhuvandas Patel organised, which became Amul Dairy. Kurien was its executive head from 1950.

**Manthan.** Shyam Benegal’s 1976 feature film, financed by Gujarat milk producers at two rupees each.

**Monetisation.** Selling donated food and using the cash for a development project, which is how Operation Flood turned powder and butter oil into dairies.

**Mother Dairy.** An NDDB venture for city milk distribution. After Kurien left, critics said it was buying from private suppliers. See [Debates](debates.md).

**NDDB.** National Dairy Development Board, established in September 1965 at Anand, with Kurien as founder chairman until 1998. Its brief was to replicate the Anand pattern.

**Operation Flood.** The national dairy programme launched in 1970 and run, on the World Food Prize chronology, until 1996. Three phases. Commodity aid plus, later, World Bank loans.

**Polson.** The private dairy of Pestonji Edulji Dalal, the dominant buyer of Kheda milk before the cooperative had its own plant.

**Tribhuvandas Kishibhai Patel.** Freedom fighter, associate of Sardar Patel, founder-chairman of the Kaira union, and Kurien’s partner. He shared the 1963 Ramon Magsaysay Award. Kurien called him the man whose happiness was infectious. Nirmala Kurien said her father loved him as a father.

**Vidya Dairy.** A working dairy set up in 1994 so that dairy-technology students could train on a modern line.

**White Revolution.** The common name for the rise in Indian milk under the cooperative system and Operation Flood. Amul and the World Food Prize both call Kurien its father.
