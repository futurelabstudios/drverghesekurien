# Videos

These links were checked by asking YouTube for the public title and channel. Nothing was downloaded. A film upload whose channel is not the archive, the broadcaster, or an institution he built is not listed, even if the title looks right.

## He is speaking, or the institution is speaking

**[In Conversation with Dr. Verghese Kurien](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ihGCwpRKOXU)**  
Channel: Prasar Bharati Archives (DD Archives).  
Why it matters: a long interview held by the public broadcaster’s archive, not a clip channel. This is the first place to hear his voice and his own sequence of the story.

**[In Conversation Today — Dr. Verghese Kurien](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SxEZtFB3jbM)**  
Channel: Rajiv Mehrotra.  
Why it matters: a sit-down interview by a documentary maker whose public-service films are a known body of work. Useful beside the Doordarshan archive copy, because the questions are different.

**[NDDB Corporate Film — English](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fU619BE1_oc)**  
Channel: National Dairy Development Board.  
Why it matters: the board’s own telling of 1946, the milk strike, Shastri’s visit, and the idea that gifted powder and butter oil were sold to build cooperatives. Treat it as an institutional account. The numbers in the narration are the board’s, and they are set next to other figures in [Impact](../impact.md).
