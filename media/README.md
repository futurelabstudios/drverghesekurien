# Media: photos, videos & documents

| Folder | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| [`photos/`](photos/) | Photographs (JPG/PNG, under 5 MB each — compress larger files) |
| [`videos/`](videos/) | **Links only** (YouTube, Vimeo, archive.org, etc.) — never upload video files |
| [`documents/`](documents/) | Letters, memos, clippings, programme booklets (PDF or scans) |

## Every item needs a catalogue entry

For each photo or document, add a Markdown file next to it using [`_TEMPLATE.md`](_TEMPLATE.md) with:

- **What** it shows and **who** is in it
- **When** and **where**
- **Source / owner** — who took it or owns it
- **Rights** — your permission to share it, and the licence

## Rights — please read

- Only upload what **you own** or have **written permission** to share.
- Press photos, Amul/NDDB/IRMA official material and film stills are usually copyrighted — link to them instead of uploading.
- Photos of private individuals need their consent (or their family's).
- Maintainers will remove anything on request from a rights holder.
