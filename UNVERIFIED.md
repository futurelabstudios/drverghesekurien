# Stories and details left out

A famous tale is omitted when this research could not open a page that tells it in a checkable way: a named witness, a journalist who heard it, or an institution publishing its own record. Paraphrases of Kurien’s books were not used as a back door, except for one episode that M. S. Sriram quotes with a page number, which is in [Stories](stories/anecdotes.md).

If you have a link that fills any row, the way to send it is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Well-known episodes not told here

**Shastri’s “nothing here is special except the people.”** Many retellings, including pages that track Kurien’s autobiography, have the Prime Minister say that Anand’s soil, climate, and buffaloes were ordinary, and then ask what made the cooperative famous. Harish Damodaran’s 2024 report and the *Times of India* account of Ajarpura confirm the night of 30 October 1964, the host, the feed plant, and the letter to chief ministers. They do not carry that conversation. A long extract of the autobiography on a private blog was not used. Searches: “Lal Bahadur Shastri visit Anand Kurien,” the Indian Express long read, the NDDB Ajarpura speech, the *Times of India* diamond-jubilee report.

**What he told Tushaar Shah about Nehru, Kosygin, Morarji, and Shastri.** Shah says that during Operation Flood II planning, Kurien would call him in and tell hilarious stories about those men. Shah does not tell the stories. Searches: Shah’s 2026 remembrance on Vrikshamandir and on LinkedIn. A 1979 visit by Alexei Kosygin is widely repeated in reference works. No official or news page confirming the visit was opened, so the visit is not in the timeline.

**Nestlé in Switzerland, about 1956.** A common version says Kurien was told that Indians could not handle condensed-milk technology, went home, and had imports cut once Amul could make the product from buffalo milk. The Magsaysay citation confirms that the Anand plant made condensed milk and buffalo-milk powder. It does not tell the Swiss meeting. Searches: Amul’s life of Kurien, the Magsaysay page, obituary interviews. No fetched page gave the meeting in the teller’s words.

**He never drank milk.** A *Business Line* profile of 9 September 2012 says he disliked milk and that it was never a favourite food. That is in [Life](life.md). The stronger claim, that he never drank it at all, was not adopted. A *Telegraph* headline of 10 September 2012 (“Kurien didn’t drink milk, he lived it”) reads as a pun and was not opened in full. Two hospital stories in [Stories](stories/anecdotes.md) are about nurses trying to get him to drink milk when he was dying. They are not evidence about his ordinary diet.

**The Tata Steel apprentice who faked the time in the register.** A “Short Tata Stories” post says the teller heard, at an IRMA graduation, that Kurien often repeated a Jamshedpur lesson: a late apprentice was dismissed for writing the wrong time, not for being late. The post does not name the person who told it that day, and it is not Kurien on the page. Searches: the LinkedIn text beginning “India’s milk man in steel country.” Left out until someone names the speaker.

**King Birendra’s request for ghee.** Rasheeda Bhagat says a former commerce secretary, whom she does not name, told her that a government-to-government request from the King of Nepal still had to be cleared by Kurien. The secretary is anonymous. The story is not in the anecdotes chapter. Source of the mention only: [*Business Line*, 13 September 2012](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/doodh-ka-doodh-kurien-style/article23032255.ece).

**A confrontation in a minister’s office.** Sriram quotes a rough passage from *I Too Had a Dream* (his page 75) in which Kurien abuses a man he thinks is lying to a minister. The passage is copyrighted dialogue from a book this archive did not read. It is not paraphrased. [Sriram’s essay](http://joy-of-books.blogspot.com/2006/04/kurien-personal-essay.html).

**Eight hundred NDDB employees resigning together.** Sriram mentions this as a moment of solidarity during a controversy over Operation Flood. He does not cite a news report. No second source was opened. Not treated as fact.

**Narendra Modi and the funeral.** Bhagat writes that Modi, then chief minister, did not come to pay respects, and that relations had soured after Kurien spoke about smaller government. A *Times of India* headline of 11 September 2012 is cited in reference works. The article was not opened. A claim about a living political figure needs the article, not the headline. Left out of [Debates](debates.md).

**The five-year bond.** A search snippet of drkurien.com said he was to serve five years in Anand. The Amul page that was actually fetched says “bond period” and a relieving order at the end of 1949. The *Business Line* profile says a two-year bond. The five-year figure is not used.

**Starting pay.** Bhagat’s recollection of his telling is Rs 350 a month. The *Business Line* profile of 9 September 2012 says Rs 275. Both are reported in [Life](life.md). Neither is silently preferred. A third figure was not found.

**Pittsburgh, metallurgy, and nuclear physics.** He told Bhagat he studied those subjects in Pittsburgh and cheated the dairy scholarship. Amul, GCMMF, and the World Food Prize say Michigan State University, mechanical engineering, 1948, with dairy as a minor. The conflict is described in [Life](life.md). Pittsburgh is not adopted as the campus.

## Biographical details not confirmed on an institutional page

These appear in some web biographies, including pages this research only saw in snippets. They are not in [Life](life.md).

- Wedding on 15 June 1953, and the name Susan Molly Peter before marriage. Seen in a snippet of [drkurien.com](https://drkurien.com/biography/). Amul names his wife as Molly and does not give the date.
- Schooling at Diamond Jubilee Higher Secondary School, Gobichettipalayam.
- A wish to join the army as an engineer, dropped for TISCO.
- Boxing for Loyola, as distinct from the general statement that he was athletic. A colleague’s memory that a private secretary said both he and Kurien had boxed for their colleges is on Vrikshamandir and was not tied to a named published witness beyond that recollection. Not used.
- The chancellorship of Allahabad University is mentioned by the World Food Prize without years. The fact of the title is in [Work](work.md). The dates are not.

## Vrikshamandir stories not yet used

The site’s [index](https://vrikshamandir.com/dr-v-kurien-stories-and-anecdotes/) points to more contributed tales (a steep ramp at the IRMA auditorium, an elevator in Mumbai, a reception desk in Bangalore, a cricket innings). Several of those URLs were not fetched, or a guessed URL returned nothing. They are omitted rather than half-told. A correction that quotes the contributor’s name from the page itself can add them.
