# Books and films

References only. This archive does not reproduce chapters, scripts, or lyrics.

## What he wrote, and what was written about him

**Verghese Kurien, *I Too Had a Dream*.** His autobiography. M. S. Sriram reviewed it on 17 April 2006 and cited page numbers from the edition he was reading. One episode from that review is paraphrased in [Stories](stories/anecdotes.md). The book itself was not opened for this archive. Sriram says it is plain-spoken, mostly about the work, dedicated to Kurien’s grandson, and thin on private life.

[Sriram’s essay](http://joy-of-books.blogspot.com/2006/04/kurien-personal-essay.html)

**M. V. Kamath, *Milkman from Anand* / *The Milkman from Anand*.** A biography. Readers of Sriram’s essay name it in the comments. It was not opened here. Bibliographic details should be taken from a library catalogue, not from this page.

Other titles that appear in secondary bibliographies — including *An Unfinished Dream*, *The Man Who Made the Elephant Dance*, Ruth Heredia’s *The Amul India Story*, and an Amar Chitra Katha comic — were not opened. They are listed in [UNVERIFIED.md](UNVERIFIED.md) so that a later editor can check them, not as works this archive has described.

## Films

**Documentaries Shyam Benegal made for Kurien before the feature.** Benegal told *The Hindu* he made *Operation Flood 1* and *Operation Flood 2*. The BBC, in 2024, says Kurien pushed him to make several documentaries on Operation Flood and on rural marketing. Copies and running times were not verified here.

[*The Hindu*, “Milkmen turned producers”](https://www.thehindu.com/features/magazine/milkmen-turned-producers/article6180423.ece)

**Manthan (The Churning), 1976.** Directed by Shyam Benegal. Script by Vijay Tendulkar. Music by Vanraj Bhatia. 134 minutes. A fictional account of a dairy cooperative being born in a divided Gujarat village, drawn from the Anand movement. Farmers financed it at two rupees each. The full episode is in [Stories](stories/anecdotes.md).

Cast named by the BBC: Girish Karnad, Smita Patil, Naseeruddin Shah, Amrish Puri, Kulbhushan Kharbanda, Mohan Agashe.

Benegal told *The Times of India* that Karnad was chosen to play Kurien. The BBC describes the Karnad character as a city veterinarian walking into village politics. Both descriptions are his interviewers’ or the film’s. The film is not a documentary of Kurien’s life.

A restored print premiered at Cannes in May 2024. Shivendra Singh Dungarpur led the restoration, with Benegal and cinematographer Govind Nihalani. The BBC reports that the sound negative had been destroyed and that the picture negative was damaged by fungus.

National Award: the BBC says the film won one. A later report of Benegal’s remarks says two — best Hindi feature film, and best screenplay for Tendulkar. Both statements are linked below.

- [BBC, 18 May 2024](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-asia-india-69014789)
- [*Hindustan Times*, Benegal, 9 September 2012](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india/how-a-farmers-servant-painted-the-nation-white/story-w5muyu4dpzbC3Huk5XYRbK.html)
- [*Times of India*, Benegal to Subhash K. Jha](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/hindi/bollywood/news/manthan-made-on-rs-2-donations/articleshow/16348081.cms)
- [*Hindustan Times*, on the farmers in the theatres](https://www.hindustantimes.com/entertainment/bollywood/when-shyam-benegal-shared-how-farmers-helped-in-making-manthan-a-success-they-all-would-come-on-bullock-carts-101734966912625.html)

## Advertising

Benegal told *The Times of India* that he met Kurien in the 1960s while working at the Advertising and Sales Promotion Company, run by Sylvester da Cunha, which handled Amul. The long life of the Amul topical cartoons is a separate history. It was not researched page by page here.
